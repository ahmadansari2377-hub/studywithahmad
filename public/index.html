                          09 · Get the app

                          Take it everywhere.

                          Install Study with Ahmad on your phone or PC. It opens in its own window with its own icon, and your planner stays in sync when you sign in. Load shedding or no internet? It keeps working offline and syncs when the net is back.

                          Android

                          For Android 7 and newer. A small download (about 2 MB).

                          Download APK
                          1. Open the downloaded file.
                          2. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
                          3. Tap Install, then open Study with Ahmad.

                          Windows PC

                          Windows 10 and 11. Tiny download (80 KB), opens in its own window.

                          Download for Windows
                          1. Open StudyWithAhmad.exe.
                          2. If Windows shows “protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.
                          3. A shortcut is added to your Desktop and Start menu.

                          iPhone & iPad

                          No download needed. Add it to your Home Screen from Safari.

                          1. Open this site in Safari.
                          2. Tap the Share button.
                          3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
