Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Study with Ahmad is a free study planner for Punjab Board Matric and Intermediate students. We keep only what the planner needs to work, we never sell data, and there are no ads.

Using it without an account

Everything you add (class, tasks, timetable, syllabus ticks, study sessions, flashcards) is saved only in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

What we save when you make an account

WhatWhy
UsernameTo sign you in. We don't ask for your real name, phone number or email.
Password and recovery codeStored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody, including us, can read them.
Your plannerClass and group, tasks, timetable, exams, syllabus progress, study sessions and flashcards, so they appear on every device you sign in on.
Your internet address (IP) for wrong sign-insTo block password guessing. Deleted after one day.
Daily count of AI questionsTo keep the free 40-a-day limit.

Things you choose to share

  • Leaderboard (off unless you switch it on): your username, class, the city you type, and this week's focus minutes are shown to other students.
  • Study rooms (off unless you switch it on): while your timer runs, others in the room see your username, subject and minutes left.
  • Ammi-Abbu link (off unless you make one): anyone with the link sees your username, class, study minutes for the last 7 days, streak, syllabus percentages, next exam, badge count and the app's marks estimate. Never your tasks, notes or chats. Making a new link or switching it off stops the old one at once.
  • Reminders (off unless you switch them on): your browser's push address is saved so we can send one short study reminder a day. Switching reminders off deletes it.
  • Feedback: the message you send, and the contact details if you choose to add them.

AI Study Buddy, paper checker, page-to-cards and practice papers

When you ask Study Buddy a question, make an AI plan or practice paper, check an answer, or send a photo of a question, a book page or your answer sheet, that text (and photo) is sent to Google Gemini to write the answer, together with your class, the subject and your recent messages in that chat. We don't save your questions or photos. Google handles them under its own Gemini API terms. Please don't put personal details in your questions.

Where it is kept

The website runs on Vercel and the database on Neon (PostgreSQL), both on secure (HTTPS) connections.

Deleting your data

  • Clear everything (bottom of the app) deletes your planner from this device and from your account.
  • To delete your account completely, send a message through the Feedback button in the app with your username, and it will be deleted.
  • Export backup gives you your own copy of your planner as a file at any time.

Children

The app is made for school and college students. We don't collect names, phone numbers, emails or locations, and we don't show ads or track you across other websites. Page visits may be counted anonymously (Vercel Analytics, which uses no cookies) to see how many people use the app.

Questions? Use the Feedback button in the app ("Koi masla? Batayein"). If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
